# Arbitrage-Free Data Driven Option Pricing
### *IISER Pune, Jun 2026 - Present*

Ongoing work to be submitted as a Master's Thesis in May 2027. Supervised by Prof. Anindya Goswami from the Mathematcs and Data Science Departments at IISER Pune, the project aims to study and extend literature on machine learning based approaches to option pricing with a particular emphasis on arbitrage-freeness. A major focus so far has been an investigation into the use of Neural SDEs to learn arbitrage-free derivative pricing models for arbitrary payoffs. A secondary objective is to develop a framework to quantify the extent of arbitrage allowed by any given model and use this a a second metric along with pricing accuracy to compare the strengths of well established models in the literature.
